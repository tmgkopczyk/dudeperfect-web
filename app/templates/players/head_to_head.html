Battle statistics: Unique battles won counts each battle once per player, even if both individual and team first-place results exist. Win rate = unique battles won ÷ recorded appearances. Team wins are credited to recorded members of winning teams.

Wheel Unfortunate / Fortunate: Counts how many times each player was selected by the wheel and how many verified Wheel events they hosted. Hosting appearances include characters portrayed by the players. Wheel events and episodes are counted separately because an episode may contain multiple Wheel events. Wheel Fortunate is counted as a Wheel variant. Being selected does not necessarily mean the player hosted or spun the wheel.

Cool Not Cool: Items presented counts the products or experiences each player introduced during Cool Not Cool. Cool and Not Cool votes received are counted from votes on that player's presented items. Super Cool requires five Cool votes, while Super Not Cool requires five Not Cool votes. Cool-majority and Not Cool-majority items are determined by comparing the number of Cool and Not Cool votes received. Votes cast represent how each player voted on items, regardless of who presented them. Special vote values such as Both, Suspended, and N/A are excluded from Cool and Not Cool vote totals.

Statistical leaders: Higher values are highlighted for positive performance statistics, while lower values are highlighted for negative outcomes. Neutral statistics do not receive trophies. Tied leaders with nonzero values are highlighted equally.

Betcha: Challenges presented counts the Betcha challenges introduced by each player. Successful and failed challenges are determined by the recorded outcome. Challenge success rate is calculated as successful challenges divided by challenges with known outcomes. Agree predictions indicate that a player expected the challenge to succeed, while Disagree predictions indicate that they expected it to fail. Correct predictions match the recorded outcome. Prediction accuracy is calculated from predictions with known outcomes. Players without recorded challenges or predictions display a dash instead of a percentage.

Get Crafty: Segment appearances count every recorded Get Crafty event in which a player participated. Challenges participated counts unique competitions. Multi-part challenges, such as Egg Drop Parts 1 and 2, are counted as one competition. Completed challenges are competitions with a recorded winner. Challenges won and lost are determined by the recorded final winner. Win rate is calculated as challenges won divided by completed challenges. Second-place, third-place, and podium finishes are determined by recorded final placements. Average placement is calculated from recorded final placements, with lower values indicating better results. Players without eligible results display a dash for win rate or average placement.
